What to expect

Come as you are

There’s no dress code and no pretense at Lakewood — just a warm welcome, honest worship, and a seat saved for you.

For the kids

Children’s Church runs during the worship service for ages 3 through 9, and nursery facilities are available for moms with little ones.

Lifted in song

Our choir, directed by Jennifer Statzer, leads the congregation in hymns and songs that have carried this church for generations.

Can’t make it in person?

Our Sunday worship service is live streamed on Facebook — join the service from your couch, your car, or across the country.
