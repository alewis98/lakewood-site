Every dollar given to Lakewood is used to glorify God’s name — supporting worship, families, and outreach here in Ringgold and around the world.

Thank you for visiting our website to learn more about Lakewood Community Church — and thank you for donating to our church.

Your donations are very important to our ministry and the outreach of our church. Every dollar donated is used to glorify God’s name, and your generous giving is greatly appreciated.

Since 1949, Lakewood has given a tenth of all tithes to Home and Foreign Missions — so your gift reaches far beyond our little corner of Pittsylvania County.
